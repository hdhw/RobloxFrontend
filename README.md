## About

**RobloxFrontend** is an archival project dedicated to preserving and documenting the evolution of Roblox's frontend throughout its history.

Spanning from the earliest days of Roblox in **2005** to the modern platform in **2026**, this repository brings together frontend files and resources from over two decades of development.

Whether you're interested in historical website designs, classic interfaces, or the evolution of Roblox's frontend, this collection aims to make that history accessible.

## Collection

The repository contains frontend-related files and resources discovered throughout Roblox's history.

All collected files were found through the work of the **Roblox Frontend Research Team**.

The goal is to preserve these resources for historical research, frontend experimentation, and educational purposes.

## Research Team

This collection is made possible by the Roblox Frontend Research Team, whose work involves discovering, collecting, and documenting frontend resources from different periods of Roblox's history.
